The warfront

When the hosts close in.

A whole front to hold. One vessel to move.

Hollow War brings Hollow Wake’s combat into a single arena. Thread a path through the horde while your Arts fire, then spend your Ultimate when a gap is about to close.

The undead host
Risen Ghouls and Skeletons crowd your path. Watch for Wraiths and Marrow Liches behind them.
The demon host
Imps and Hellhounds pour onto the field alongside Demon Brutes and Abyssal Demon Lords.
Field controls

WASD or arrow keys to move. Q toggles auto-aim. Space or Shift triggers your Ultimate. Tab opens the Build & Arts Inspector.

Buildcraft

Give an Art a different future.

Equip up to four Arts. Each can reach rank eight and take two compatible Inscriptions. A change to one Art can reshape how you handle a whole wave.

Take Firebolt. Split Volley adds two projectiles but cuts each bolt’s damage. Add Cascade and each impact can scatter shards into the pack behind your target.

During a run, level-up choices show an estimated DPS change. Open the Inspector to see what drives it.

In development · Shared account concept

What could follow you home?

We’re exploring a shared account that could let each game remember what you did in the other. Finishing Hollow War might earn a wardrobe appearance in Hollow Wake. In the other direction, breaking a siege in the future Hollow Wake could change which host you face on your next visit to the warfront.

These are design examples. Cross-game progression is planned for the future.
